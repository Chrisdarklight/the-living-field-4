This Electroculture Antenna Installation Kit includes:

  • 1 heavy-duty galvanised steel mast-to-mast mount (fits ¾″–2″ pole/post)
  • 25 ft of 10-gauge insulated copper wire
  • Rubber and plastic insulators
  • 1 brass coupler

The kit can be used with all The Fertile Current antennas. It includes everything you need to mount your antenna except the mounting pole/post, and galvanised steel wire for traditional applications.
