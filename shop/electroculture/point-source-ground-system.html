The Point Source Radiance Ground System (PSRGS) offers solutions for those who are unable to install traditional Electroculture ground line conductor systems and adds versatility to Electroculture system installation possibilities.

The PSRGS offers an alternative to the traditional ground line conductor systems most commonly used in Electroculture. Through magnetic repulsion and varying electrical input, the PSRGS radiates an isotropic field of electromagnetic energy. This spherical field of influence has effect on both soil and atmosphere.

The PSRGS installs in the soil at the base of a mounted Electroculture Antenna and is compatible with all The Fertile Current Electroculture Antenna models.

The observed field of influence for these systems is a 25 ft. radius extending from the centre in all directions. In real application, a single PSRGS and antenna would cover a 50 sq. ft. garden if placed in the centre.

Point Source Radiance

Magnetic repulsion is created when the like poles of a magnet or magnetic array face each other and resist attraction. They generate a magnetic force of interactive fields outward from the centre point source of this repulsion.

The field is considered essentially static until electrical input is introduced, which animates the magnetic field of potential energy into an electromagnetic spherical wave of kinetic energy, with equal intensity in all directions: "isotropic," meaning the "equal path" or "balanced way." This could just as easily be the "middle way" of the Buddha or the Tao, which simply means "path."

The Wei-chi field in Qigong is an isotropic field which is more commonly referred to today as the bio-field — essentially an outward spherical electromagnetic field of information, powered by the magnetic field produced by the heart vortexing supercharged ions and the electrical impulses via the sun expressing the will of Heaven.

This field can be cultivated and fertilised, but also harvested from, and that is important to remember, especially in a culture where the few cultivate but the many reap.

The wave field from the point source radiance is most intense at the centre, with dissipating intensity as it undulates away from the source. It will become less observable with the original method of detection, and it is the method of detection that must adapt at that point, because the wave field simply does not disappear.

In fact, no energy or matter can disappear in our universe. Energy and matter can reorganise and certainly become unrecognisable, but that is far from ceasing to exist, and that energy and matter will forever be entangled on the quantum level with that source and will always remember home.

This wave will speak of its home wherever it travels, and spread the frequency of its origin unceasingly.
