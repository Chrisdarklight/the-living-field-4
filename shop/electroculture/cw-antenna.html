The Split-Ring Resonator

The 15cm SRR is mounted opposite the brass telluric collection rod, with the gap of the ring oriented to the North upon installation. The single SRR — also known as a Lakhovsky coil — is an oscillating circuit that uses the gases within the air gap as its point of capacitance. The angle of the ring is adjustable for "tuning" via polarity, and the ring itself is made of copper and brass with a copper mount and stainless steel set screw.

Installation notes

Mounting hardware is sold separately in the Antenna Installation Kit, and most installations also benefit from a dedicated ground kit for a cleaner, more reliable connection back to the earth — see our ground systems. If this is your first installation, we'd genuinely recommend the training course below before you mount anything; grounding well is one of the biggest factors in whether a system completes its circuit and does the work it's meant to.

Every CW1+ is built the way this equipment has always been built — by hand, by people who use it themselves, not on a mass production line. What you're installing is a small, deliberate piece of a much older relationship between growers and the current that was always moving through their land.
