What's inside the CW2+

The CW2+ — "Asha" — uses the same core technologies as the CW1+ in a shorter, dual-spear form built for lower-mount installations: an atmospheric collector, a tuned resonant circuit and telluric ground connection, a voltaic stack and paramagnetic basalt core. This breaks down what each part does.

The Split-Ring Resonator

The 15cm SRR is mounted opposite the brass telluric collection rod, with the gap of the ring oriented to the North upon installation. The single SRR — also known as a Lakhovsky coil — is an oscillating circuit that uses the gases within the air gap as its point of capacitance. The angle of the ring is adjustable for "tuning" via polarity, and the ring itself is made of copper and brass with a copper mount and stainless steel set screw.

Installation notes

Mounting hardware is sold separately in the Antenna Installation Kit. If this is your first installation, we'd genuinely recommend watching the installation video below before you mount anything; grounding well — typically a length of galvanised steel wire run in a direct line to your earth connection — is one of the biggest factors in whether a system completes its circuit and does the work it's meant to. If a direct south-to-north ground run isn't possible on your site, our Point Source Radiance Ground System is a wire-free alternative.
