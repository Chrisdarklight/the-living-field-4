These magnet arrays are stacked in phase and thickly coated in beeswax. The ground wire is threaded through the centre of the magnetic stack prior to laying into the trench. By increasing the magnetic field of the ferrous conductor, these boosters amplify the electric signal as it vortexes around the wire and throughout the rhizome layer of the soil. Magnetic south is marked for easy installation (red dot faces south, toward the antenna).
