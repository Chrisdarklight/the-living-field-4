The Elixir Field Electroculture System Amplifier makes the most of your TFC Electroculture antenna system. Installing between the antenna and ground conductor connection, the EF can be quickly added to an existing Electroculture system by simply splicing in the amp using the provided couplers and insulated lead wire. By multiplying the low voltage signals generated by the antenna/conductor system, the Elixir Field enables maximum efficiency and effectiveness of your equipment.

The passive circuit is presented in a custom PCB (printed circuit board) and includes a crystal oscillator/resonator as well as a series of diodes and capacitors which compound and direct the potential difference between Heaven & Earth.

The circuit board also states the maxim "Heaven advances with unceasing resolve, Earth yields with faithful embrace." This statement represents the fluxing potentials of cosmic and terrestrial phases that reconcile into the force of electric life.

Meticulously crafted and mounted inside a weatherproof lock box, the Elixir Field is simple to install and adds dynamic potential to any Electroculture system.
