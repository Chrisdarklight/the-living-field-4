The Cloud Sea Aqua Inductor is a dynamic treatment device for all flowing water applications. Employing electromagnetic induction, the Cloud Sea imbues bio-harmonic voltage into your agricultural or household water by design.

In addition, the choke coil effect of the internal induction assemblage reduces EMI, or electromagnetic interference, generated by man-made and cosmic sources, as well as mitigating RFI, radio frequency interference from wifi routers, cellular networks and transmitters. While all the signals may not be harmful, they are simply unknown and are typically an undesired addition to water supplies.

Faraday's Law of Induction states that a varying magnetic field gives birth to an electromotive force, which directs electric currents in a nearby conductor, like water. This motional induction process "excites" atoms within the treated water and, as the atoms relax back to "ground" state, light is emitted which catalyses voltage (chi energy) to be generated and radiated.

The Cloud Sea Aqua Inductor can be quickly connected in-line to any standard garden hose, fitted into irrigation systems, and plumbed directly into hydroponic pump systems or reservoir filling stations. Every scale of agriculture and gardening can utilise this versatile and effectual water treatment device to charge, vitalise and protect our most indispensable and precious resource.

The increased magnetic field of Cloud Sea water attracts "ground" state nitrogen as well as oxygen. Water with higher levels of dissolved oxygen (DO) is advantageous for all plants without exception. In a hydroponic growing system, where plants are rooted in water, the higher DO level is even more critical, as it enhances root growth, fights disease, and catalyses nutrient uptake, since plant roots require oxygen for respiration. Just like all other living things, plants breathe.

The increased DO in Cloud Sea water also assists in preventing detrimental bacteria and disease pathogens that can damage roots.
