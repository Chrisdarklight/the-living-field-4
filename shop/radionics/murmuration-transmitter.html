The Murmuration Divine Order Transmitter is a user-friendly radionic broadcaster for balancing all terrains.

Applying homeopathic principles, the Murmuration broadcasts beneficial vibrational rates to support the natural functions of life, and can be used to support physical health, agricultural agendas, emotional/spiritual states, environmental factors and beyond.

The Murmuration's input is fed by silica chip cards that store and transmit vibrational data to the machine, to be broadcast into the local environment.

Coverage area of the broadcast depends on the immediate physical barriers and the intensity dial setting on the machine. Think of the intensity dial like the volume knob on a radio: the higher the dial setting, the louder the music is at the source, and the further away it can be heard.

Included with the Murmuration is a set of 5 program cards based on the five elements in Traditional Chinese Medicine: Water, Wood, Fire, Earth and Metal. These element cards relate to organ systems in the body, emotions, seasons, planets, colours and more.

Along with the Murmuration Divine Order Transmitter, you will receive the 5 Elements card collection, and a grounding plug for indoor use, with a grounding rod adapter for outdoor/field use.

*The Transmitter can be used outdoors but is not designed to be left outside in rain or other harsh weather.
