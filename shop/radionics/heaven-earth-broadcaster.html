How it works

Radionics works through what's sometimes called eloptic energy — carrying some characteristics of electricity, some of optical light. Much like tuning to a radio station, the selected sample and reagent patterns broadcast through the soil once placed in the circuited well and powered by the potential difference between Heaven and Earth. A field map is included with every broadcaster to set the boundaries of intent and limit the broadcast range to the plot it's meant for.
