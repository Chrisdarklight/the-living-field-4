Chai Blend

The ultimate blend to add to your morning coffee, smoothie or tea. This superfood chai has a sweet cocoa aroma with a kick of spice — a warming, grounding ritual drawing on Ayurvedic and traditional Mesoamerican ingredients brought together in one cup.

Read moreShow less →

Our Chai Blend brings together Organic Lions Mane, Organic Cocoa and Organic Black Ginger extract — mushroom, cacao and root brought together in a single warming powder. Cocoa has been prized since the Maya and Aztec, who called it the "food of the gods"; black ginger (Kaempferia parviflora) has a long history in Thai traditional medicine; and lion's mane has a place in East Asian culinary and tonic traditions. Together, this blend is sustainably harvested and made without fillers or preservatives.
