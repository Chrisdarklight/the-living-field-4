Research & sources

Cordyceps sinensis has drawn a genuine body of published research, spanning traditional-use surveys, animal studies and human clinical trials into its effects on exercise performance, the airways and reproductive health. A selection of studies is linked below for anyone who wants to dig deeper — presented as further reading, not as claims about this specific product.

  • Traditional uses and medicinal potential of Cordyceps sinensis of Sikkim, review — PMC
  • Cordyceps sinensis and cordycepin in male reproduction, review — PubMed
  • Effect of Cs-4 on exercise performance in healthy older subjects, double-blind placebo-controlled trial — PubMed
  • Cordyceps sinensis promotes exercise endurance capacity by activating skeletal muscle metabolic regulators, animal study — PubMed
  • Cordyceps sinensis Cs-5 enhances sexual function in normal and impotent models, research abstract — The FASEB Journal

These are links to independently published research on Cordyceps sinensis generally — not studies of this specific product, and not medical advice. See "What we don't claim" below.
