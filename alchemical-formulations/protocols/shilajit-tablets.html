Shilajit Tablets

Formed over millions of years through the geological compression of organic matter within the Himalayan mountains — shilajit is one of the earth's most primordial alchemical substances. Known across Ayurvedic tradition as the Destroyer of Weakness and the Conqueror of Mountains, it carries over 84 minerals in their ionic form alongside fulvic acid, humic acid and a mineral intelligence unlike anything else in nature.

This protocol explores the history, Ayurvedic lineage and traditional wisdom of shilajit — paired with an 8 week observation practice and a downloadable PDF study guide.
