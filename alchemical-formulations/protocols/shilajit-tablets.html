Formed over millions of years through the geological compression of organic matter within the Himalayan mountains — shilajit is one of the earth's most primordial alchemical substances. Known across Ayurvedic tradition as the Destroyer of Weakness and the Conqueror of Mountains, it carries 84 of the 102 minerals the body needs in their ionic form, alongside fulvic acid, humic acid and a mineral intelligence unlike anything else in nature.

Shilajit 88 is our partner Cultivate Elevate's own cold-pressed extraction, standardised to 88% fulvic acid — one of the highest concentrations available in a cold-pressed tablet. This protocol pairs it with an 8 week observation practice and a downloadable PDF study guide exploring its history and Ayurvedic lineage.

What we don't claim
