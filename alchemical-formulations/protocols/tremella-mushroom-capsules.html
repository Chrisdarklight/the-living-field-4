Organic Tremella Mushroom Capsules

"The beauty mushroom."

Known across traditional Chinese medicine as the "beauty mushroom," Tremella fuciformis — also called snow fungus or silver ear — has a long history of use for moisture, radiance and long-term vitality. Its translucent, jelly-like fruiting body is prized for a polysaccharide content that traditional use associates with hydration and skin resilience.

Organic Tremella Mushroom Capsules is our partner Cultivate Elevate's own organic extract, standardised to a 15:1 extraction with 30% beta glucans from 100% fruiting bodies — no mycelium fillers, no grain starch. Tremella naturally contains amino acids involved in the body's own collagen production, alongside compounds traditionally associated with hyaluronic acid levels and skin moisture. It's also one of the richest known food sources of Vitamin D.

Research & sources

Tremella fuciformis has drawn a genuine body of published research into its polysaccharide content, spanning skin, cognitive and general antioxidant effects. A selection of studies is linked below for anyone who wants to dig deeper — presented as further reading, not as claims about this specific product.

  • Structure, bioactivities and applications of Tremella fuciformis polysaccharides, review — PubMed
  • Polysaccharides and UV-induced photoaging, reducing skin water and collagen loss — ScienceDirect
  • Efficacy and safety in individuals with subjective cognitive impairment, randomised controlled trial — PubMed
  • Anti-fatigue and anti-hypoxia effects of Tremella extract, animal study — PubMed
  • Protective effect of Tremella polysaccharides against radiation-induced damage, animal study — PubMed

These are links to independently published research on Tremella fuciformis generally — not studies of this specific product, and not medical advice. See "What we don't claim" below.
